## Description

Demonbacks are mutant gorillas, the result of fiendish experimentation. They can generate electricity to shock their adversaries. They roam dense, primal forests in packs, their malevolent presence and sparking fur serving as an eerie reminder of the supernatural forces lurking within the natural world.

## Monster Parts

- **Demonback Hide:** Can be used to craft armor with natural lightning resistance.
- **Accumulator Organ (Demonback):** Can be used as a component in enchanting or crafting to generate lightning-based effects.
