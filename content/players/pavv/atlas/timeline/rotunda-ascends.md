The Ebon Aristocracy quite literally rose during the early Third Age, heralded by the sudden appearance of the Rotunda—an obsidian palace said to have grown from the corpse of a long-dead demon prince named Veltherion. Descendants of cursed bloodlines, including tieflings and drow, embraced the decadent ideals of the dead prince and forged a new society of wealth, secrecy, and power. Though distrusted, their influence spread swiftly through dark alliances, political treachery, and control of forbidden magic.
