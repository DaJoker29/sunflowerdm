## Description

This nightmarish spawn of the deep boasts twisted, tentacle-like appendages that writhe beneath the churning waves. Its malevolent presence is often found lurking near the coasts, waiting for unsuspecting beach-dwellers and divers to feed its insatiable appetite.

## Monster Parts

- **Abyssal Essence:** A viscous substance extracted from the beast’s core, possessing latent magical energy. When refined by a skilled alchemist, it can be used to create potent potions or enchantments.
- **Lurker’s Ink Sac:** Harvested from one of its massive tentacles, this ink sac holds a unique substance with an uncanny ability to adhere to surfaces. Scribes and craftsmen often use it to create waterproof scrolls or ink that bonds exceptionally well to various materials.
