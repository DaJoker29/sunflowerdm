> **_In Pavv, the still die slowly and the reckless die loudly—only the wise live long._**  
> **_And those who survive know one truth: Pavv cannot be conquered—only endured._**

## Paradise or Prison

To the outside world, Pavv is a land of mystery—an untouched frontier where fortune-seekers venture and vanish without a trace. But many who live here view it as a prison. The island repels outsiders, and those who call it home find themselves bound here by forces unknown. But to others, it is the only home ever known. Some dream of escaping its shores, while others seek to uncover its secrets. Whatever their ambitions, the people of Pavv have learned that survival demands more than strength; it demands ingenuity, perseverance, and the wisdom to know when to turn back.

## Civilization and Wilderness

Civilizations have risen up behind the walls of stone and timber, carving out pockets of safety amidst an unforgiving wilderness. Generations have lived and died beneath the island’s strange skies, building kingdoms, founding faiths, and forging traditions of their own. Yet beyond the roads and city walls, much of Pavv remains untamed. Ancient ruins lie forgotten beneath the jungles and forests, strange creatures roam the wilds, and countless mysteries await those brave, or foolish, enough to seek them.

## The Six Rooted Truths

- **Pavv’s wild paradise** was sculpted over centuries from barren rock by the archdruid **_Obree_**.
- **Obree vanished without a word or sign,** and no druid since has matched their power.
- **Pavv’s Pantheon** formed gradually as more inhabitants arrived with their respective faiths, evolving into the living gods we see today.
- **Violent seas and unseen barriers** enforce geographical isolation, believed to be divine in origin.
- **Outsiders still arrive**—like the Jiksidurian Exiles and the Eight Pilgrims—but they often bring ruin, war, and old-world curses.
- **Pockets of civilization exist**, but the wilds beyond the city walls remain untamed.

## Choice & Consequence

Pavv is a dynamic setting that responds and shifts to player choices. Actions have meaningful consequences that cannot always be foreseen. Uncertainty and discovery are a part of the adventure.
