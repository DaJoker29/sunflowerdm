In my opinion, dungeons are meant to be scary, traumatizing even. Which is why I don't show dungeon maps to players, I let them build it in their imagination. Again, we want the players to feel immersed in the theater of the mind, not treating it like a board game. Having said that, having a map and a key are integral to providing that thrilling experience.

I usually build my dungeons in [Procgen Arcana's One Page Dungeon Generator](https://watabou.github.io/dungeon.html) or [Donjon's Random Dungeon Generator](https://donjon.bin.sh/fantasy/dungeon/). I cycle through options and adjust until I find a map that fits the theme and concept I have in mind.

Once I have a map selected, I go about building the key. Basically, filling out each room with monsters, loot, lore, and clues the players might find. When I run games in Roll20, I like to keep this map on my screen with tokens to represent the party and show their progress, along with marking the movements of monsters and NPCs as well.

{{< figure src="dungeon-dm.png" alt="A dungeon map with tokens" caption="Dungeon - DM's Perspective">}}

However, the players see none of this. Instead, I show them a simple visual and provide a blank area for their collective notes.

Where as regional maps require a good amount of worldbuilding, these require a good amount of imagination. The devil is in the details: most of which you aren't going to be able to put on a map. So having a secondary document to track those details is vital.

{{< figure src="dungeon-players.png" alt="A visual graphic with blank space" caption="Dungeon - Players' Perspective">}}

An important thing to note about running dungeons in this way: **you are effectively running theater of the mind, even if there is a map in front of you**. Thus, it is essential that your narration and descriptions are clear enough to allow players to make smart choices.

_Note: when the party enters battle, I will switch to a separate battle map—especially for a boss fight. This set up in exclusively for traversing the dungeon._
