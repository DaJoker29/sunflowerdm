## Cooperation & Party Expectations

As we play this game, we want everyone to share the spotlight, respect other players’ contributions, and make mindful adjustments to ensure everyone has a fun, enjoyable session. Characters need to cooperate and work together towards shared goals. While there is room for some player vs. player (PvP) interactions on occasion, they are best planned out ahead of time with consent from both parties or avoided altogether.

## Safety Tools

This setting includes themes of death, evil, and the brutality of the wilds. These are some of the tools used to ensure a safe, shared game space.

***Lines.*** This game will not contain sexual violence, abuse, incest, or pedophilia.

***Veils.*** Consensual sexual acts will fade to black—no explicit scenes.

***X-Card / “Pause”.*** At any time, feel free to express your discomfort with the current scene (verbally or nonverbally) to the DM, and we will proceed accordingly.

***Open Door Policy.*** Anyone can take a break or step out at any time.

Players are free to raise concerns and revisit boundaries at any time.

## Scheduling & Attendance

***Session Length.*** Unless otherwise stated, typical session length is 3 hours with a 15-minute break in the middle.

***Cancellation.*** Cancellation is no problem. I simply ask that you let us know as soon as possible.

***Minimum Players.*** Generally, I will not run sessions with fewer than 2 players.
