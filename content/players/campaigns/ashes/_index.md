> _The Gravesreach Towers have begun rebuilding themselves, just as the Black Crucible has violently erupted, ending years of empty dormancy and peace in the Ashen Wastes._

Centuries ago, the lich Maldrathar the Undying brought down the kingdom of Shademire. Even after his defeat, the land remained cursed. Now a long-dormant volcano has erupted, poisoning a river that neighboring lands depend on, and the ruined towers on the road to his fortress are rising again.

An expedition from Solara has made camp at the edge of the Ashen Wastes. Scholars, clergy, and treasure seekers all want to know what has awakened. They differ on what to do about it. You’re one of the adventurers they’ve called in to find out what’s happening.

## The Campaign

_Ashes of the Undying_ is a weekly D&D 5e campaign set in Pavv's Deadlands. Expect wilderness exploration, ruined sites, tactical combat, and rival patrons with their own reasons to send you into the Wastes. Your party decides which leads to follow, and some trails may take you beyond the Ashen Wastes.

- **Tables:** Sunday afternoons and Wednesday evenings, Pacific time.
- **Levels:** Start at level 3. The campaign is planned through level 8.
- **Where we play:** Roll20 for maps and gameplay, Discord for voice and between-session planning.
- **Experience:** New and experienced players are welcome.

### Two crews, one Expedition

Each weekly table has its own regular party. Both crews play in the same continuity and use the same Expedition camp, Roll20 game, regional map, and Discord Mission Board. A waystation one crew secures can become the other crew’s base for a later mission. A lead you pass along might bring back news that changes your own plans.

## Joining the Expedition

Create a character from Pavv with a reason to brave the Wastes and a willingness to work with the party. Your character might answer to a patron, have family downstream of the poisoned river, or hope to make a living recovering what Shademire left behind. Bring an idea you’d like to explore and leave room for it to change through play.

Before your first session, we’ll meet for a Session 0 or a brief voice orientation to talk through your character, the campaign, and table expectations. You’ll need free Discord and Roll20 accounts and a working microphone.

You may maintain up to two characters. Choose one at the start of each mission. That character is the one you play for the whole mission, even when it spans multiple sessions. Each character advances independently.

## Choosing and Running Missions

The Mission Board in Discord lists Expedition assignments, known dangers, and posted rewards. Look there for work, follow up on scouting reports, or propose an objective of your own. The party can choose what to pursue at the end of a session or work it out in Discord before the next one.

- **Danger ratings** are the Expedition’s best assessment of the threats ahead. Use the skull rating and suggested levels to decide how much risk to take and how to prepare.
- **Patron assignments** are optional objectives a character can pursue alongside the crew’s main mission. They can earn extra rewards or improve your standing with the patron who offered them.
- **Preparation** gives the party one camp activity to choose before a mission when there’s an opportunity to prepare. The options are in Discord. Reply with the party’s choice so the GM can prepare the benefit before play.
- **Field reports** let the other crew know what you’ve found. Share discoveries, warnings, and leads through Discord so they can inform future missions and map updates.

## Standing & Rewards

**Standing.** When you complete an assignment that offers standing, your character earns 1 standing with that patron or camp group. Standing is tracked separately for each character and each group in the relevant Roll20 camp handout. The ranks are Unknown (0), Associate (1), Trusted (2), Favored (4), and Champion (6). You can see Associate and Trusted benefits in the handouts. Favored and Champion benefits are revealed as you unlock them.

**Gold and gear.** Mission postings list gold rewards per player. If significant items are found on missions, the party decides who keeps it. If several characters want the same significant item, they can bid gold into the party’s pool for it. Items the party does not wish to keep can be sold to the Quartermaster. The Quartermaster handles camp sales and auctions, and players can buy, sell, and trade with one another through the supply tent channel.
