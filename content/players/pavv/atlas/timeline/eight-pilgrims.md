Another exception to Pavv's strict isolation policy was made when a convoy of ships known as ***the Eight Pilgrims arrived on the southern shores***. The inhabitants were mostly dwarf, elf, and orcish folk fleeing prejudices in their oppressive homeland. These Pilgrims soon proliferated across the land, building their own kingdoms and city-states, while inadvertently bringing many **curses of the Old World** with them.
