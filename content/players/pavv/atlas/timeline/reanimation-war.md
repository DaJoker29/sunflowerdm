For a time, war engulfed the land as the evil lich launched a continental assault with an army of undead. The conflict, later known as **the Reanimation War**, united the native inhabitants of Pavv and these Jiksidurian exiles. Though it came at a great loss, their alliance eventually destroyed the mad king and his forces.
