## Feral Demonback

_Large fiend (Beast), unaligned_

- **Armor Class** 12
- **Hit Points** 57 (6d10 + 24)
- **Speed** 40 ft., climb 40 ft.

|     STR |     DEX |     CON |    INT |     WIS |    CHA |
| ------: | ------: | ------: | -----: | ------: | -----: |
| 23 (+6) | 14 (+2) | 18 (+4) | 9 (−1) | 12 (+1) | 7 (−2) |

- **Saving Throws** Str +9, Con +7
- **Skills** Athletics +12, Perception +4
- **Damage Resistances** fire, poison
- **Damage Immunities** lightning
- **Senses** blindsight 10 ft., passive Perception 14
- **Languages** understands Infernal and Primordial
- **Challenge** 5 (1,800 XP)

**Pack Tactics.** The demonback has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the demonback’s allies is within 5 ft. of the creature and the ally isn’t incapacitated.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** The demonback makes two fist attacks.

**Fist.** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +9 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 17 (2d10 + 6) bludgeoning damage plus 5 (1d10) lightning damage.

**Rock.** _Ranged Weapon Attack:_ +9 to hit, range 50/100 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 30 (7d6 + 6) bludgeoning damage.

**Electrify Metal (Recharge 5–6).** The demonback emits a pulse of energy which electrically charges any metal objects nearby. Each creature within 20 ft. of the demonback holding a metal weapon or wearing metal armor takes 2d8 lightning damage.

The creature must then succeed on a DC 17 Constitution saving throw or drop the object if it can. If it doesn’t drop the object, it has disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks until the start of the demonback’s next turn.

### Reactions

**Unnerving Shout.** When a creature the demonback can see starts its turn within 30 ft. of the demonback, the demonback can lock eyes and deliver a mighty roar to intimidate their foe. If the creature can see the demonback, it must succeed on a DC 17 Wisdom saving throw or be frightened until the end of its turn.

### Legendary Actions

The demonback can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature’s turn. The demonback regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

- **Attack.** The demonback makes one attack.
- **Teleport (Costs 2 Actions).** The demonback magically teleports, along with any equipment it is wearing or carrying, up to 120 ft. to an unoccupied space it can see.
- **Heal Self (Costs 3 Actions).** The demonback magically regains 11 (2d8 + 2) hit points.
