## Welcome, Dungeon Master

Whether you’re planning your next session or improvising at the table, start with whatever you need right now.

- [Random Generators]({{< relref "/dm/generators" >}}) give you names, places, and other details to build on.
- [Roll Tables]({{< relref "/dm/roll-tables" >}}) give you prompts to use during prep or at the table.
- Open **Statblocks** in the sidebar for creatures you can bring into your game, such as [Demonbacks]({{< relref "/dm/statblocks/monsters/demonbacks" >}}).
